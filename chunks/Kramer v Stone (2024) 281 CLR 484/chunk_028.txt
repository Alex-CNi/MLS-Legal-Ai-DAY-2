description” (75).
The application of equitable estoppel in this case
The elements of equitable estoppel, set out above, were plainly
satisﬁed in this case. Dame Leonie made a clear promise to David that
David would inherit the Farm upon Dame Leonie’s death. In making
that promise, a reasonable person in Dame Leonie’s position would
have expected, and Dame Leonie did expect, that David would rely
upon the promise by abstaining from any other employment. David did
