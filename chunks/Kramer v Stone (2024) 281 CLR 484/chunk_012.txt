Introduction
The respondent is a farm worker who earned an irregular and
meagre income working on a farm as a “share farmer”, sharing some
of the costs and income derived from the farm. The farm worker was
made a promise by the owner of the farm that he would inherit the
farm on the owner’s death. Without that promise, the farm worker
would have terminated the share farming agreement, obtained
employment that returned a much higher level of income, and enjoyed
a new lifestyle free from some of the hardships endured on the farm.
