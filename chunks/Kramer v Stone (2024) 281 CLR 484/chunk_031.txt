But whatever might be, or should be, the state of Australian law

Corin v Patton . There was no application to reopen any of the
reasoning in that decision.
The appellants principally relied upon two further decisions in
support of transplanting to equitable estoppel a requirement of
subsequent encouragement, after a promise, by a donor of a purported
gift. Neither decision supports that transplant.

company owed £2,000 to Mr Dyson. Mr Dyson told his wife orally,
“Y ou can have the £2,000 that I have loaned to [the company]” and

payments of interest should be made to her. No document was ever
executed by Mr Dyson before he died. No statutory assignment of the
loan ever occurred. Kitto J (with whom Barwick CJ, Menzies J and
