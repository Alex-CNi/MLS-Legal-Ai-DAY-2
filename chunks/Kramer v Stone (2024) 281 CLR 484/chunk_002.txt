Dame Leonie Kramer (“Dame Leonie”) and her husband, Dr Harry
Kramer (“Dr Harry”), owned a 100-acre farm at Upper Colo,
New South Wales, as joint tenants. In 1975, at the age of 22, David
Stone began share farming on the farm pursuant to an informal, oral
agreement with Dame Leonie and Dr Harry. There were terms of the
agreement to the effect that Mr Stone would grow crops and maintain
the farm; Dr Harry would pay all operating costs except for fuel, the
cost of which would be shared equally between Mr Stone and Dr Harry
(later in the ratio one-third/two-thirds); Mr Stone would live rent-free
in a house on the farm; and Mr Stone would receive a quarterly
retainer of $600 (later increased), and half the gross proceeds from the
sale of produce and cattle. The agreement was terminable at will. In the
